# Reactions and Equilibrium

## Puzzle 1. Race to 7

Two people take turns counting numbers starting from 1. You cannot skip numbers or repeat numbers. On each turn, a player may say **one or two consecutive whole numbers**. Whoever is first to say 7 wins.

<ThinkAboutIt>

Should you go first or second? What is your winning strategy?

</ThinkAboutIt>

## Puzzle 3. KFC & McDonald’s

KFC and McDonald’s plan to open their first store on a new street. There are two popular locations:

- **Center:** the exact middle of the street.
- **Side:** the point one-quarter along the street.

Both companies must decide **at the same time**: “Shall we open at Center, or Side?” Neither knows what the other will choose.

| KFC \ McDonald’s | Center | Side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Center** | KFC +5, McDonald’s +5 | KFC +10, McDonald’s +2 |
| **Side** | KFC +2, McDonald’s +10 | KFC +7, McDonald’s +7 |

<ImageFigure src="/images/economics/reactions-equilibrium/kfc-mcdonalds.png" alt="A payoff diagram for KFC and McDonald's location choices" />

<ThinkAboutIt>

Which location should each company choose? What would you expect the other company to do?

</ThinkAboutIt>

## Puzzle 4. The Green Eyes

On an island live **three people with perfect logic**.

<ImageFigure src="/images/economics/reactions-equilibrium/green-eyes-islanders.jpg" alt="Three islanders on the green-eyes island" />

Rules:

1. Everyone can see the eye colour of all other people.
2. No one can see their own eye colour.
3. No one on the island may talk about eye colour.
4. All people are perfectly rational. Everyone knows everyone else is perfectly rational.
5. If anyone works out that they have green eyes, they **must leave the island at midnight the same day**.
6. If they cannot be certain, they stay.

<ImageFigure src="/images/economics/reactions-equilibrium/green-eyes-visitor.jpg" alt="A visitor speaking to the islanders" />

Facts:

- All three islanders have green eyes.
- No one knows their own eye colour.

One day, a visitor comes and speaks publicly to everyone:

> “I see at least one person with blue eyes.”

<ThinkAboutIt>

On which night do they leave the island? Why?

</ThinkAboutIt>

## Puzzle 5. The Best Wand

You represent your wizard school in a fight against two other wizards:

- **Wizard from Newt-niz School:** His wand turns people into fish. Hit rate: **70%**.
- **Witch from Leib-ton School:** Her wand turns people into stone statues. Hit rate: **90%**.

<ImageFigure src="/images/economics/reactions-equilibrium/best-wand-wizards.jpg" alt="Three wizards preparing for a contest" />

The three take turns attacking in this fixed order: **You → Newt-niz Wizard → Leib-ton Witch → You → …** Anyone who gets hit is out of the fight.

It is your turn first. You must pick one wand from three choices:

| Wand | Power | Success rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bannekar** | Trap one target with vines | 60% |
| **Gaussian** | Turn one target into a tree | 80% |
| **Noether 9000** | Send one target far away to a mountain top | 100% |

<ImageFigure src="/images/economics/reactions-equilibrium/wand-options.jpg" alt="The three wand options" />

<ImageFigure src="/images/economics/reactions-equilibrium/wand-payoffs.png" alt="A diagram of the wizard contest choices" />

<ThinkAboutIt>

Which wand should you choose? What strategy gives you the highest chance to win?

</ThinkAboutIt>

## A note for the next move
